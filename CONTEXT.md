# Brackstone marketing site

The public website for Brackstone Digital at brackstonedigital.co.uk. It explains what Brackstone does for small UK businesses and turns visitors into teardown bookings and free trials.

## Language

### Pages

**Homepage**:
The main page of the site, aimed at organic and referral visitors. It sells all three pillars and leads with the teardown.
_Avoid_: Landing page, home

**Ads page**:
A focused page built for one paid search intent. It sells only the AI receptionist, repeats the search in its headline, and leads with the free trial. Paid traffic lands only on ads pages, never on the homepage. A trade's ads page is the only page the site has for that trade.
_Avoid_: Landing page, LP, campaign page, industry page, trade page

**Estate agent**:
A business that sells homes, lets homes, or both. One ads page serves all of them.
_Avoid_: Letting agent page, property page

**Search intent**:
The thing a person typed into Google that an ads page answers, such as "AI receptionist for garages". One ads page serves one search intent.
_Avoid_: Keyword, vertical

**Dashboard**:
The Brackstone web app at dashboard.brackstonedigital.co.uk, built in a separate repo. Customers sign in there to follow their free trial and use the receptionist. It checks and records every trial request.
_Avoid_: Portal, app (on its own). Say "custom dashboard" for the thing we build for a client, and "Kanban board" for where tickets live.

### Offer

**Pillar**:
One of the three equal things Brackstone sells: enquiries on every channel, admin automation, and custom dashboards and integrations.

**Custom dashboard**:
A reporting and control screen Brackstone builds for one client's business. It is part of the third pillar and is not the Dashboard.
_Avoid_: Dashboard (on its own)
_Avoid_: Service, product line

**AI receptionist**:
The voice agent that answers a business's phone calls, books work and passes on urgent calls. It sits inside the enquiries pillar and is the most popular thing Brackstone sells.
_Avoid_: Voice bot, chatbot, call agent

**Teardown**:
A free 15-minute call where Brackstone looks at what a business does by hand and says what could run on its own. It is the homepage's main call to action.
_Avoid_: Consultation, discovery call, audit

**Free trial**:
A card-free, 7-day trial of the AI receptionist with 50 trial minutes, set up by Brackstone. The 7 days start at go-live, not when the trial is requested. It ends at 7 days or 50 trial minutes, whichever comes first. Every business gets the same trial; garages also see workshop mode. It is requested on the site's get-started page and is the main call to action on every ads page.
_Avoid_: Demo, free account, self-serve trial

**Trial request**:
What a business submits on the site's get-started page to ask for a free trial. The dashboard checks and records it; Brackstone reviews each one and sets the receptionist up within the onboarding window.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, lead, application

**Onboarding window**:
Up to 7 days between a trial request and go-live, while Brackstone sets the receptionist up. Brackstone aims to finish sooner, but the site promises no more than this.
_Avoid_: Setup time, waiting period, lead time

**Go-live**:
The moment a trial receptionist starts answering the business's calls. The free trial's 7 days are counted from here.
_Avoid_: Activation, launch, start date

**Trial minute**:
One minute of call time answered by the AI receptionist during a free trial, counted to the second. Time spent on a call after it has been passed to a person does not count.
_Avoid_: Credit, call (on its own)

**Forwarding confirmed**:
The point at which a business's calls are proven to reach its receptionist, shown by the first real call that arrives through the business's own number. It is detected, not ticked by hand.
_Avoid_: Connected, set up, installed

**Setup waiver**:
The setup fee is not charged when a business moves from a free trial to a paid plan during the trial or within 48 hours of it ending.
_Avoid_: Discount, free setup, promo

**AI receptionist plan**:
The paid plan for any business: from £99 a month with 150 minutes and a £149 one-off setup. Prices carry no VAT.
_Avoid_: Starter, basic plan

**Garage edition**:
The paid plan for garages: £249 a month with 400 minutes and a £299 one-off setup, including workshop mode and vehicle lookup from the registration.
_Avoid_: Growth, garage plan, pro

**Demo line**:
A real phone number a visitor can ring to hear the AI receptionist take a call.
_Avoid_: Demo number, test line

### Measurement

**Primary conversion**:
A trial request the dashboard has accepted. This is the number that tells us an ad worked.
_Avoid_: Lead, sign-up, conversion (on its own)

**Secondary conversion**:
A sign of interest on the marketing site: a tap on a free trial button or on the demo line, before any trial request is sent. It is counted, but never treated as proof that an ad worked.
_Avoid_: Micro-conversion, click, lead

### Trial emails

**Nurture sequence**:
The emails a business receives from trial request to a few days after its trial ends, sent from Saeed. Most are chosen by what has happened on the trial, such as forwarding confirmed or calls answered, not only by the date.
_Avoid_: Drip, campaign, newsletter

**Service email**:
A nurture sequence email about the trial the business asked for, such as its go-live, its usage or its pause. It does not need marketing consent but still carries an unsubscribe link.
_Avoid_: Transactional email, notification

**Marketing email**:
A nurture sequence email that encourages the business to buy, such as trade proof or a check-in. It is sent under the soft opt-in from the trial request and stops the moment the business unsubscribes.
_Avoid_: Promo, newsletter, cold email

**Check-in**:
A personal email Saeed sends himself a few days after a trial ends. The system drafts it; it never sends on its own.
_Avoid_: Follow-up (on its own), automated personal email

### Honesty

**Built**:
A piece of past work Brackstone has actually delivered, described without naming the client. Only builds on the approved list may be mentioned.
_Avoid_: Case study, client, portfolio

**Trial promise**:
What the free trial does for a business with standard setup: it answers calls, takes the caller's details, texts the business a summary and uses a UK number. For garages it also includes workshop mode and vehicle lookup from the registration. An ads page leads with the trial promise and nothing more.
_Avoid_: Feature, capability

**Setup offer**:
Something Brackstone has built and can set up for a customer, but which a free trial does not include, such as booking viewings into a diary, connecting a CRM, or a custom call flow. An ads page may mention it only as a separate, clearly labelled line.
_Avoid_: Add-on, upgrade, premium feature

**Illustrative**:
A label for any transcript, name, score or example that is made up to show how something works. Anything not real must carry it.
_Avoid_: Example, sample, mock
