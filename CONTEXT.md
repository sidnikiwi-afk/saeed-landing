# Brackstone marketing site

The public website for Brackstone Digital at brackstonedigital.co.uk. It explains what Brackstone does for small UK businesses and turns visitors into teardown bookings and free trials.

## Language

### Pages

**Homepage**:
The main page of the site, aimed at organic and referral visitors. It sells all three pillars and leads with the teardown.
_Avoid_: Landing page, home

**Ads page**:
A focused page built for one paid search intent. It sells only the AI receptionist, repeats the search in its headline, and leads with the free trial. Paid traffic lands only on ads pages, never on the homepage. A trade's ads page is the only page the site has for that trade.
_Avoid_: Landing page, LP, campaign page, industry page, trade page

**Estate agent**:
A business that sells homes, lets homes, or both. One ads page serves all of them.
_Avoid_: Letting agent page, property page

**Search intent**:
The thing a person typed into Google that an ads page answers, such as "AI receptionist for garages". One ads page serves one search intent.
_Avoid_: Keyword, vertical

**Dashboard**:
The Brackstone web app at dashboard.brackstonedigital.co.uk, built in a separate repo. Customers sign in there, and it hosts the free trial request page.
_Avoid_: Portal, app (on its own). Say "custom dashboard" for the thing we build for a client, and "Kanban board" for where tickets live.

### Offer

**Pillar**:
One of the three equal things Brackstone sells: enquiries on every channel, admin automation, and custom dashboards and integrations.

**Custom dashboard**:
A reporting and control screen Brackstone builds for one client's business. It is part of the third pillar and is not the Dashboard.
_Avoid_: Dashboard (on its own)
_Avoid_: Service, product line

**AI receptionist**:
The voice agent that answers a business's phone calls, books work and passes on urgent calls. It sits inside the enquiries pillar and is the most popular thing Brackstone sells.
_Avoid_: Voice bot, chatbot, call agent

**Teardown**:
A free 15-minute call where Brackstone looks at what a business does by hand and says what could run on its own. It is the homepage's main call to action.
_Avoid_: Consultation, discovery call, audit

**Free trial**:
A card-free trial of the AI receptionist, requested through the dashboard's trial request page. It is the main call to action on every ads page.
_Avoid_: Demo, free account

**Demo line**:
A real phone number a visitor can ring to hear the AI receptionist take a call.
_Avoid_: Demo number, test line

### Measurement

**Primary conversion**:
A free trial request that is actually submitted on the dashboard. This is the number that tells us an ad worked.
_Avoid_: Lead, sign-up, conversion (on its own)

**Secondary conversion**:
A sign of interest on the marketing site: a tap on a free trial button or on the demo line. It is counted, but never treated as proof that an ad worked.
_Avoid_: Micro-conversion, click, lead

### Honesty

**Built**:
A piece of past work Brackstone has actually delivered, described without naming the client. Only builds on the approved list may be mentioned.
_Avoid_: Case study, client, portfolio

**Trial promise**:
What the free trial does for a new customer with no setup work, such as capturing viewing requests. An ads page leads with the trial promise and nothing more.
_Avoid_: Feature, capability

**Setup offer**:
Something Brackstone has built and can set up for a customer, but which a free trial does not include, such as booking viewings into a diary. An ads page may mention it only as a separate, clearly labelled line.
_Avoid_: Add-on, upgrade, premium feature

**Illustrative**:
A label for any transcript, name, score or example that is made up to show how something works. Anything not real must carry it.
_Avoid_: Example, sample, mock
